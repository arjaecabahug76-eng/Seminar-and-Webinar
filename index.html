COMPUTER HARDWARE BASICS

Date: September 8, 2026
Duration: 6 Hours
Organizer: Cisco Networking Academy

Covers fundamental concepts of physical computer components, system architecture, hardware identification, and essential maintenance practices.

Key Takeaways

This module provided a complete, hands-on overview of core computer hardware architecture, deconstructing how physical micro-components interact to process and store data efficiently. Detailed discussions covered Central Processing Unit (CPU) operation, Random Access Memory (RAM) speed dynamics, motherboard chipsets, and modern storage interfaces such as NVMe SSDs. In addition to component identification, the training highlighted systematic diagnostic protocols for hardware troubleshooting, emphasizing electrostatic discharge (ESD) safety practices and preventive maintenance routines to extend hardware lifespan. Mastery of standard expansion interfaces, peripheral connectivity standards, and power supply calculation rounded out the technical competencies acquired during this intensive training.

SEMINAR_05 Completed

INTRODUCTION TO GREENHOUSE GAS ACCOUNTING FOR IT

Date: September 9, 2026
Duration: 3 Hours
Organizer: Cisco Networking Academy

Measuring, tracking, and reporting carbon emissions generated by IT infrastructure under GHG Protocol standards.

Key Takeaways

Addressing environmental sustainability within technology operations, this specialized course covered the standardized accounting metrics used to quantify carbon footprints across enterprise IT infrastructure. Following the internationally recognized Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Protocol, it explained how to categorize emissions into direct fuel combustion (Scope 1), purchased electricity consumption for servers and facilities (Scope 2), and value chain supply chain footprint (Scope 3). Key discussions highlighted practical green computing techniques, such as server virtualization, power usage effectiveness (PUE) metrics in data centers, hardware lifecycle management, and systematic baseline emissions tracking to satisfy modern corporate environmental compliance standards.

SEMINAR_06 Completed

INTRODUCTION TO IoT

Date: September 8, 2026
Duration: 6 Hours
Organizer: Cisco Networking Academy

This introductory seminar provides a foundational overview of the Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem and its practical applications across modern industries. Participants explore how physical devices collect, exchange, and process data through interconnected networks to drive smart decision-making.

Key Takeaways

This course provided a comprehensive overview of the Internet of Things ecosystem, detailing the foundational four-tier architecture that links smart sensors, network infrastructure, edge computing, and cloud analytics. It highlighted how real-time telemetry from physical devices powers predictive decision-making and automated workflows across various industries. Participants examined the criteria for selecting suitable networking protocols—such as MQTT, CoAP, LoRaWAN, and Cellular—based on specific power, range, and bandwidth constraints. Finally, the session emphasized the critical role of end-to-end security, addressing essential strategies for endpoint protection, encrypted data transport, access control, and scalable over-the-air firmware management.
